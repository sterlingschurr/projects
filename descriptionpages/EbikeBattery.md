# Custom LiFePO4 Electric Bicycle Battery
48V Ebike battery created out of individual 20ah LiFePO4 cells with battery management and custom case. This battery was created to power my ![custom electric bike](ElectricBike.md), so go check that out as well!

## The Problem
Ebike batteries come in wide varieties and often high costs. You can order ones that are very cheap, but they might have safety concerns and often last only a year or two. You can avoid those issues with the right battery, but that will often come at a high cost. All of these batteries are often difficult to repair, to the point of the manufacturer actively discouraging and preventing it.

## The Solution
![Concept diagram](media/EbikeBatteryconceptdiagram.jpg)
Lithium Iron Phosphate (LiFePO4) batteries are cheap, very stable, and fairly easily sourced. Making use of many small capacity cells in series, I can build a battery pack of my own design that has a customizable amount of power, full repairability, and very low cost.
