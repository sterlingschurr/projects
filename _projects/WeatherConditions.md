## The Problem
I have certain hobbies that require certain weather conditions. Pickleball requires low wind and warm-enough temps. Kayaking needs mid-low wind and a warmer range of temps. Flying a drone likes loweer wind, doesn't need a specific temp, but demands no precipitation. Remembering all of these requirements and checking the weather against them is a lot of mental work that could all be automated.

## The Solution
![Concept diagram](/assets/images/WeatherConditionsconceptdiagram.jpg)

The solution is making an app that can evaluate these criteria for me an present them in a digestable pattern. The idea is to create new activities with certain weather criteria behind them. With acceptable ranges, the app can then show you on a given day which activities would be best suited to the weather that day. Then, instead of looking at a forecast of just weather, you can look at the forecast of activities you'd like to do.

I plan to create an Android App to accomplish this.

While I haven't completed much of the work yet, this project will likely use a lot of AI to make it. I am trained as a computer engineer, and this is squarely software engineer territory. I'm not sure how much purely-AI code I will need, but it will certainly be helping me along.
