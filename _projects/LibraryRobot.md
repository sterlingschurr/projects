Inexpensive Wheeled Robot commissioned by the David O. McKay Library for teaching robotics workshops.

## The Problem
The David O. McKay library approached me about making a device for teaching robotics. The library frequently teaches various beginner-level technical workshops as a public service. The issue is that robotics are often expensive, and the budget for these workshops was extra low. The device had to be low-cost, easy to produce, easy to use, and fun enough to pay attention to.

## The Solution
![Concept diagram](assets/images/LibraryRobotconceptdiagram.jpg)
Fortuitously The library had 3D printers that I could use. I designed a robot with 4 wheels and steering, an Arduino core on a breadboard, and battery power. All parts of the robot other than the electronics were designed by me in Fusion360 and could be fully 3D printed on the library printers. They could also be assembled without tools, keeping the cost very low.
